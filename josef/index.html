About

Josef Ginnerskov is a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute for Analytical Sociology and a junior fellow at the Swedish Excellence Centre for Computational Social Science (SweCSS) at Linköping University. His research examines how society shapes culture and knowledge, combining social theory and analytical philosophy with machine learning and large text corpora.

He holds a PhD in sociology from Uppsala University, where his dissertation used computational text analysis of doctoral dissertations to ask whether sociology has Kuhnian paradigms. The thesis was awarded the Swedish Sociological Association's prize for the best doctoral thesis in sociology 2024–2025. His current work asks how social forces shape the ideas expressed by scientists, writers and state officials, and what kind of field computational social science is becoming.
