About

Mette Ginnerskov-Dahlberg is Associate Professor (docent) in Sociology of Education at Uppsala University. She is an anthropologist by training and holds a PhD in European Studies from Aarhus University.

Her research focuses on student migration, using ethnographic and longitudinal methods with a regional focus on Western Europe and the South Caucasus. She is particularly interested in the lived experience of migration, the link between geographical and social mobility, and the labour market integration of student migrants. Her recent work follows Indian medical students in Georgia, examining how migration, education and future mobility pathways intertwine.
